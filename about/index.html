Built on two decades of getting companies paid

R2Cash Global was founded to give businesses of every size access to the order-to-cash leadership that large multinationals rely on, and to put practical AI to work on the tasks that slow cash down.

Our story

R2Cash Global LLC. A bilingual team, working in English and Spanish.

For more than 20 years, our leadership has been responsible for the part of the business that turns sales into cash, leading billing, accounts receivable, credit, commercial collections, cash application and reconciliation teams serving customers in the United States, Canada, Latin America, Europe and Asia-Pacific. That experience includes roles at Marriott, World Fuel Services, IATA, Discovery Inc., Oracle, Sanofi and Citibank.

Across those roles, our leadership has overseen more than $250 billion billed and collected with a 99.99% collections rate and bad debt below 0.01%.

That work has rarely been about one system or one fix. It has meant redesigning how sales, operations and finance hand work to each other; tightening credit and billing so fewer invoices are disputed; building collection routines that are persistent without damaging customer relationships; and giving leadership numbers they can trust.

Our leadership has also led finance transformation and automation work, building billing and collections workflows with system integrations and structured approvals on platforms including NetSuite and SuiteBilling, Salesforce, SAP S/4HANA FI-AR and HighRadius.

R2Cash Global brings that experience to clients directly. The AI agents the company builds, including the AI Receptionist and Hector the Collector, are designed around how real finance and customer-service teams work.
