Privacy policy

How R2Cash Global LLC handles information you share with us. Effective October 2, 2026.

Who we are

R2Cash Global LLC ("R2Cash", "we", "us") is a business-to-business consulting and automation company based in Miami, Florida. This policy covers r2cash.com and the messages you send us by email, phone, text or WhatsApp.

Information you give us

When you contact us, we collect what you choose to provide: your name, company, business email, phone number, preferred contact method, the service you're interested in and a description of your needs.

Our contact form is for business inquiries. Please don't send Social Security numbers, bank or card details, account passwords, medical information or invoice files through it.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the site. The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address when a page loads. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or analytics that profile individual visitors.

How we use information

  • To reply to your inquiry and arrange consultations
  • To provide services you request from us
  • To keep records of our business communications
  • To protect the website and our business from spam, fraud and misuse
  • To meet legal obligations

Calls, texts and WhatsApp

We contact you by phone, text or WhatsApp about your request only if you ask us to or give consent on our form. Consent is not a condition of doing business with us. You can withdraw it at any time by telling us, or by replying STOP to a text message. Message and data rates may apply. WhatsApp is a service of Meta, and messages you send through it are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting, a form-delivery service that forwards contact-form messages to our inbox, and email. They may use it only to provide their services to us.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public
  • With your permission

Client data

Information we process on behalf of our clients during consulting or automation work, such as their customer or invoice records, is governed by our written agreements with those clients, not by this policy.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information you send us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep inquiry information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information you've shared with us, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email info@r2cash.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Visitors outside the United States

We are based in the United States, and information you send us is processed and stored in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version and its effective date will always be on this page.
