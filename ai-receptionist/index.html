A receptionist in your customers' language, for the calls you can't take

The R2Cash AI Receptionist answers your business line in any language you configure, finds out why someone is calling, books the appointment or takes the details, and passes urgent calls to your team. It's designed for busy hours, lunch breaks and after closing.

Talk to us about the AI Receptionist Call +1 (786) 999-2008

What it does on every call

Each capability is configured to your business. We confirm which calendar, CRM and phone integrations fit your setup during design.

  • Answers inbound calls

    Greets callers with your business name and handles common requests immediately.

  • Any language

    Can be configured in any language, so every caller is served in the language they speak.

  • Identifies the reason for the call

    New appointment, reschedule, question, quote or urgent issue, then routes accordingly.

  • Captures caller details

    Name, company, phone, email and reason for calling, recorded accurately for follow-up.

  • Schedules appointments

    Books available times and offers alternatives when the first choice is taken.

  • Answers approved questions

    Hours, location, services and policies, using only answers you approve.

  • Creates records

    Turns each call into a lead or service record your team can act on.

  • Covers after hours

    Answers evenings and weekends when your office is closed, on the schedule you choose.

  • Escalates to a person

    Transfers or flags urgent, sensitive or complex calls using rules you define.

What a call can sound like

A short example of an after-hours call to a dental office. The receptionist books the visit without asking for medical details, and a person follows up on anything urgent.

Configured to avoid sensitive patient information

For dental and medical offices, the receptionist can be set up to collect only scheduling and contact details and to direct clinical questions to your staff. Ask us about your specific privacy requirements; we review them with you before launch.
