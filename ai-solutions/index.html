How we set up an agent

Every business is different, so agents are configured rather than switched on. Available integrations and features depend on your systems; we confirm what's possible during design.

  1. Discover

    Map the calls, invoices or leads the agent will handle, and the rules it must follow.

  2. Design

    Write approved answers and scripts, define what gets escalated and to whom, and agree success measures.

  3. Implement

    Configure the agent, connect the systems in scope, and test with your team before going live.

  4. Optimize

    Review conversations and results, refine scripts and expand to new workflows.

Guardrails come standard

Agents should make your business look more professional, not less.

  • Approved answers only

    Agents respond from information you approve. They do not invent prices, policies or account details.

  • Human escalation

    Disputes, complaints, urgent issues and anything outside the script go to a person on your team.

  • Minimal data collection

    Agents can be configured to avoid collecting sensitive information such as patient health details or payment card numbers.

  • Honest about being AI

    Agents never misrepresent who they are, and disclose that they are AI assistants when asked.

  • Reviewed for your use case

    Rules for calling, texting, recording and consent vary by industry and location. Each configuration should be reviewed for your use case and jurisdiction before launch.
